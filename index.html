Frequently Asked Questions

How many work hours are in a year for a 40-hour work week?

There are 2,080 standard work hours in a 52-week calendar year (40 hours × 52 weeks = 2,080 hours). Divide your annual salary by 2,080 to find your exact unadjusted base hourly wage.

What is the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) overtime rule?

Under federal FLSA rules, non-exempt hourly and salaried employees who work more than 40 hours in a single 7-day workweek must receive overtime pay at a rate not less than 1.5 times their regular hourly rate.

What deductions come out of a gross paycheck?

Every US worker pays 7.65% in mandatory FICA taxes (6.2% Social Security up to the wage base cap and 1.45% Medicare with no cap). Additionally, federal income tax withholding, state income tax (0% in FL, TX, WA, NV, TN, WY, AK, SD, NH), and health insurance/401(k) contributions are deducted.
